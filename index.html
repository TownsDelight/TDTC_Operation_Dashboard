Manage user access

Admin, Chef 1 (Manager), and Chef 2 (Assistant) are fixed accounts. You can enable or disable the Chef 1 and Chef 2 accounts here — anyone signing in with a disabled account will fall back to Viewer. Usernames and passwords cannot be changed from this screen.

Master Archive

Wrap up the month: download everything from Production Planning and Saved Packages (plus recipes, stock and history) as one Excel workbook, keep a copy that can be re-opened any time in a read-only viewer, and optionally clear the boards for the new month.

After saving, reset for the new month?
Production Planning batches stay as they are. (Resetting Production Planning above also removes its batches from Dispatching.)
The Calendar (events, food tastings, production dates, open/closed marks), recipes and inventory stock are never reset.
